General Instructions

  1. The player will take a turn rolling the dice.
  2. Once rolled, the number shown is how many tiles the player will jump.
  3. The landed tile determines the difficulty of the question the student will answer.
  4. All questions are multiple choice. Easy questions are worth 10 points, Average 15 and Difficult 25.
  5. Each student must answer a total of 10 questions.
  6. Checking is done after the whole game, on the results board.
